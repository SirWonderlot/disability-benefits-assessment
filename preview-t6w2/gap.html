Guaranteed Access Program (GAP)

What is the Guaranteed Access Program?

The Guaranteed Access Program (GAP) is designed to provide a one-time, tax-free payment to a person's loved ones at the time of their passing. The program is intended to help cover final expenses.

Key information

  • Eligibility: The program is available to individuals under the age of 80 who are living with a long-term medical condition.
  • Guaranteed Access: No medical tests or medical records are required to qualify for the program.
  • Benefit Payment: A tax-free lump sum payment (minimum $10,000) is paid to a person of the participant's choosing at the time of their passing.
  • Maturity Benefit: If the participant lives to the end of the term, the funds are paid directly to them instead.
  • Guaranteed Payout: The funds are always paid out, either to a loved one upon passing or to the participant if they outlive the term.
  • Monthly Contribution: This program is designed to be financially accessible for people at all income levels.
