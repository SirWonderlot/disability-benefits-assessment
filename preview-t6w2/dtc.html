What is the Disability Tax Credit?

The Disability Tax Credit (DTC) is an application to the Canada Revenue Agency. It confirms that a long-term medical condition affects your everyday life. You need to be approved for it before you can receive the benefits above.

If you live with a long-term medical condition, the DTC is worth applying for. Once you're approved, it opens the door to several benefit programs that can support you for years to come.

Your next step

Apply for the Disability Tax Credit. Once you qualify, you can apply for each of the benefits above.
