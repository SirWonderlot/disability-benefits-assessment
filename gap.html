Guaranteed Access Program (GAP)

What is the Guaranteed Access Program?

The Guaranteed Access Program (GAP) is designed to provide a one-time, tax-free payment (minimum $10,000) to a person's loved ones at the time of their passing. The program is intended to provide peace of mind for everyone involved. The funds can be used however they wish, including for final expenses or to pay outstanding debts.

Key information

  • Eligibility: The program is available to individuals under the age of 80 who are living with a long-term medical condition.
  • Guaranteed Access: No medical tests or medical records are required to qualify for the program.
  • Benefit Payment: A tax-free lump sum (minimum $10,000) is paid to a person of your choosing at the time of your passing. The benefit payment is often significantly higher.
  • Maturity Benefit: If you live to the end of the term, the funds are paid directly to you (minimum $10,000 and often substantially more). The funds are paid out 100% of the time. They go either to a person of your choosing in the event of your passing, or directly to you if you outlive the term.
  • Guaranteed Payout: The funds are always paid out, either to a loved one upon passing or to you if you outlive the term.
  • Monthly Contribution: This program is designed to be financially accessible for people at all income levels. The cost is very low, typically between $1 and $1.50 a day.

How the program works

  1. Step 1

    Determine your benefit amount

    The minimum benefit amount is $10,000, and it is often significantly more. Your benefit amount is determined with the help of a Benefits Specialist.

  2. Step 2

    Complete a quick questionnaire

    After completing the questionnaire, you will be able to speak with a Benefits Specialist who will help you better understand the program. They will show you your benefit amounts, which are paid to the person or persons of your choosing in the event of your passing, or paid directly to you when the plan matures.

  3. Step 3

    Enroll in the program

    Your Benefits Specialist completes the application for you so that you can participate in the program.

  4. Step 4

    The death benefit is paid out

    If you pass away during the term, your tax-free lump sum benefit amount is paid to the person or persons of your choosing.

  5. Step 5

    The maturity benefit is paid out

    If you live to the end of the term, the funds are paid directly to you as a tax-free lump sum payment.

Are you interested in the Guaranteed Access Program?

Benefits Review session During your Benefits Review session, a Benefits Specialist will explain the program, review your estimated benefit amounts, answer your questions and identify any additional benefits you may be entitled to receive. Before scheduling your Benefits Review session, please answer a few brief yes/no questions. Your responses will help determine your benefit amounts (minimum $10,000 and often substantially more). After you have completed the brief questionnaire, you will be able to select a day and time that is convenient for you. Time to complete: less than 2 minutes
