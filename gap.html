Guaranteed Access Program (GAP)

Available to residents of British Columbia and Ontario only.

The Guaranteed Access Program (GAP) is offered through iA Financial Group for people living with prolonged medical conditions. It provides a one-time, tax-free payment to a person's loved ones at the time of their passing. It is intended to help cover final expenses during a very difficult time for a person's loved ones.

Key information

  • Eligibility: Available to individuals aged 6 months to 80 years who are living with a long-term medical condition.
  • Guaranteed access: No medical tests or medical records are required to qualify.
  • Benefit payment: A tax-free lump sum (minimum $10,000) is paid to a person of your choosing at the time of your passing.
  • Maturity benefit: If you live to the end of the term, the funds are paid directly to you instead. Minimum payout is $10,000.
  • Guaranteed payout: The funds are always paid out — either to a loved one upon passing, or to you if you outlive the term.
  • Monthly contribution: The program is designed to be financially accessible for people at all income levels. Cost is typically $1 to $2 a day and is guaranteed to never increase.

Your next step: the health questionnaire

The Guaranteed Access Program starts with a short health questionnaire. Your answers determine the benefit amount you qualify for.

  1. Complete the health questionnaire. Start it now using the button below, or later from your Next Steps page at the end of this assessment.
  2. Book your review. When you finish, choose a time to meet with a Disability Benefits Specialist.
  3. Review with your specialist. Because this is an important program, your specialist reviews your questionnaire with you to make sure everything is accurate, confirms your benefit amount and its cost, and assists you with additional disability benefits programs (if applicable): the Disability Tax Credit Certificate, Canada Disability Benefit, Registered Disability Savings Plan, Canadian Dental Care Plan, and Canada Pension Plan Disability.
