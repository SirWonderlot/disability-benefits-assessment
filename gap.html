Guaranteed Access Program (GAP)

The Guaranteed Access Program (GAP) is offered through iA Financial Group for people living with prolonged medical conditions. It has three parts: a death benefit, a maturity benefit and a guarantee that the funds are always paid out.

No medical is required to participate. A person living with a long-term medical impairment has guaranteed access to this program.
