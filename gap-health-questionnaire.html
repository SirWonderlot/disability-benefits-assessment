Guaranteed Access Program

Health questionnaire

The next step for the Guaranteed Access Program (GAP) is a short health questionnaire.

  • Takes less than 3 minutes
  • No medical tests or medical records needed
  • Your answers determine the benefit amount you qualify for

The questionnaire opens in a new tab. When you finish you can return to your summary.
