Disability Tax Credit (DTC)

DTC approval is required for the Canada Disability Benefit, the RDSP, and many other programs, so it is often the first step.

What is the Disability Tax Credit?

The Disability Tax Credit (DTC) is a non-refundable tax credit that helps people with disabilities, or their supporting family members, reduce the income tax they may have to pay. Being approved also opens the door to other programs.

What DTC approval can mean

You don't need an income to benefit. Benefits marked No income needed are paid even if you have little or no income.
