Disability Tax Credit (DTC)

To receive the benefits below, you first need to qualify for the Disability Tax Credit.

What is the Disability Tax Credit?

The Disability Tax Credit (DTC) is a 16-page application to the Canada Revenue Agency. It measures how your long-term medical condition affects your everyday life. You need to be approved for it before you can receive the benefits below.

If you live with a long-term medical condition that affects you on a day-to-day basis, whether physical or mental, you should apply for the Disability Tax Credit.

The Disability Tax Credit certificate is a requirement for many benefit programs. If you are approved, you may collect several benefits over a number of years.

Receiving disability benefits

To collect your benefits, you first need to qualify for the Disability Tax Credit. It is a requirement for each of these programs.

After you have qualified for the Disability Tax Credit, you will be able to receive the benefits mentioned above.

There are three different ways to apply for the Disability Tax Credit. To learn about them, you will need a short DTC Application Review session with a Benefits Specialist.

The session takes approximately 15 minutes and is conducted over Zoom, so your Benefits Specialist can share their screen and show you the three options and how to apply.

Once you have selected the option that is best for you, your Benefits Specialist will continue to assist you with your application.
